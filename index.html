Here we are in "the City of Hibiscus"—Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan Province, widely known as the warm "Land of Abundance".


Our first downtown stop is Chunxi Road and Taikoo Li. The huge climbing panda sculpture on the IFS building is the most popular landmark here. Thousands of tourists come to take photos with it every day. Modern shopping malls mix with cute panda snacks and local small food stalls.


Then we walk into Kuanzhai Alleys. They are old western Sichuan folk houses with grey stone roads and quiet wooden yards. Local elders sit outside and drink jasmine tea. You can slow down and enjoy the relaxing old Chengdu lifestyle here.


Next stop is Du Fu Thatched Cottage. It’s a quiet bamboo garden, once the home of Tang poet Du Fu. We can visit the Poetry History Hall and read thousands of stone poems on the tablets. Every corner is full of poetic beauty.


My favourite place today is Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding. Fluffy pandas lie in thick bamboo and eat fresh bamboo shoots all day. We also met colourful peacocks walking freely in the woods. Pandas are the sweet symbol of this city.


We also took a day trip to Dujiangyan, a 2,000-year-old water project. The Yuzui Fish Mouth divides the Min River to protect the plain. After sightseeing, we tasted spicy soft tofu, a special local snack.


Don’t forget Sichuan hot pot! Red spicy soup, fried snacks and marinated eggs are everywhere. Food carries the warm and lively spirit of Chengdu.


OK, so much for our eight-hour tour of Chengdu. There's a "Back" icon at the bottom of the page. Click it, choose Chongqing or New York from the menu, and start your new trip!

Hi dear visitors, I’m your tour guide Robin. Welcome to Chongqing, the famous Magic Mountain City, surrounded by Yangtze River and Jialing River.


We first arrive at Jiefangbei, the People’s Liberation Monument. It stands in the busy central shopping area. Nearby we visited chocolate art museum with a huge elephant sculpture for interesting photos.


Then we climb stone steps of Shancheng Lane and Shibati. Winding stone roads, old folk houses and colourful wall paintings show the real old Chongqing life. Standing on the high platform, we can enjoy full river views.


Liziba Light Rail is the world-famous special sight here. The train runs straight through a tall residential building. You cannot find such amazing traffic view in any other city.


When evening comes, we visit Guotai Art Centre, which local people call Chopsticks Building. Later we walk to Hongyadong. When all lights turn on, the riverside stilt houses look like a fairyland cartoon town.


Bayi Food Street is our food paradise. Spicy butter hot pot, pea noodles and glutinous fried cakes are all local classics. Strong hot flavours match warm, bold Chongqing people.


OK, that’s our eight-hour trip of mountain city Chongqing. Click the "Back" button at the bottom, pick Chengdu or New York in the tour menu, and start your next trip!

Hello everyone, I’m Robin, your tour guide. Welcome to New York, the biggest city in the United States of America. People also call it the Big Apple.


Our first stop is Wall Street. It is the world’s most famous financial centre. Many banks and big companies stand along this busy street. A bronze bull statue sits at the street corner, and thousands of visitors take photos with it every day.


Then we take a ferry to visit the Statue of Liberty. It stands on a small island in the harbour. The huge lady holds a torch high in her right hand. She is a famous symbol of freedom for Americans.


Next we walk into Central Park. It is a huge green park right in the middle of Manhattan. People run, ride bikes, row boats on the lake and have picnic here. It is a quiet place away from noisy tall buildings.


Times Square is full of bright neon screens all day and night. Crowds of tourists walk between shops and restaurants. Broadway theatres are just nearby, where we can watch wonderful musical shows.


Don’t miss the Empire State Building. We can take the lift to the top floor and enjoy the whole city view. All streets, rivers and skyscrapers spread out under our feet.


New York has different kinds of food from all over the world. We can taste American burgers, Italian pizza and Chinese noodles in one street. It is a city full of different cultures.
