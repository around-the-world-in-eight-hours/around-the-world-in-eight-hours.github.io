5Here we are in "the City of Hibiscus"—Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan Province, known as the "Land of Abundance".


Jinli Ancient Street, the world-famous old street of Western Sichuan, is right next to Wuhou Temple. There are many traditional teahouses, spicy snack stalls and folk craft shops here, where you can taste authentic Sichuan flavors and feel the charm of ancient Bashu culture.


10Further on is Chunxi Road & IFS. Every year, millions of people gather here to shop, eat hot pot and take photos with the giant panda sculpture climbing the wall. It's exciting to see the neon lights lighting up the whole street at night!


In the northwest of the city is the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding. With lush bamboo forests, gentle streams and cute pandas rolling around, it's a good place to relax and get close to these national treasures after a hard day's walking.


15When you visit Chengdu, don't miss Du Fu Thatched Cottage. It has been famous for its poetic culture since the Tang Dynasty. Have you ever heard of the poem *Thatched Cottage Broken by the Autumn Wind*?
